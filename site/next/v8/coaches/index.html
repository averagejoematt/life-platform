How it works

The same three steps, every day.

  1. My scale, wrist strap, bed sensor and food log record the day.
  2. Each morning the coaches read it, write to me, and make predictions with a date on them.
  3. When the date comes, code compares the prediction with what happened. No coach marks its own work.
