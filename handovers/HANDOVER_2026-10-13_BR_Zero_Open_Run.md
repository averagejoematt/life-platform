- **`gh pr update-branch` can say "conflicts" and still leave a merge commit on the branch** (#4871) — a lane resolving the conflict must `git fetch` first or its push is rejected.
- **Filing from a review follows the issue-filer contract:** Later is a `## Backlog rows` line, ≤10 stories per batch, and a review-filed story needs a `## Set` section or nightly hygiene reds (3 of 7 did).
- **Comment timestamps come from `date -u` / `mergedAt`, never a mental clock** — four comments were patched after a one-hour drift.
- **The reader-truth judge is nondeterministic across runs on unchanged content** (3 HIGHs per run, different pages each time); a sentence-matched ruling buys one run. Structural rulings (the page's quoted copy, window arithmetic) are the only durable fix short of re-dating EP4.
