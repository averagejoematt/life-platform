## The rules the tooling relies on

These are the parts the code and skills cite (`lambdas/content/journal_quotes.py`'s mark-time taboo gate,
`mcp/tools_journal.py`, the journal-quote and essay commands, ADR-142). They are stated here in general terms only.

**Abstract or omit, always:**
- illness, death or grief in his family, beyond "a loss in the family"
- the specifics of health-anxiety episodes (symptoms, places)
- anything about his relationship or partner
- specific cancelled personal plans or trips
- substances of any kind
- therapy, beyond "I sought professional support"
- specific events at work (his role, his team, layoffs), beyond "work upheaval"

**Safe to include:**
- that he has done this before and keeps coming back to it (never a count of attempts or resets, per the 2026-09-26
  ruling)
- that the 2024–25 run was real, and that life events derailed it (kept vague)
- that this is about coping, mind and purpose, not only weight
- the platform as both his own tool and a learning lab
- the feeling of being "an extra in my own life"

**Tone:** self-aware, not self-pitying; the insight is the hero, not the suffering; end on forward momentum.

**Why the guardrails exist:** the site is public, people close to him may read it, and doxxing and bad actors are a
real risk. The emotional truth matters; the triggering specifics can be abstracted.
