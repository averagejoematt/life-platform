How it works

Thirteen devices and apps are wired in — a scale, a wrist strap, a bed sensor, a food log, a lifting log, his phone — and eleven reported this week; one is stale and one is paused. Every figure on this page is computed by code, not by an AI, and carries its date and its count. Eight AI coaches read those figures each morning and write to him; every dated claim they make is graded later by code against the data, and the misses stay on the record. An AI drafts the weekly write-up and Matthew reviews it before it publishes. Running all of it has cost $102.74 so far in September, for one subscriber.

The code, in full: github.com/averagejoematt/life-platform
