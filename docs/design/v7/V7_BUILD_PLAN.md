### 1b. How A works, mechanism by mechanism (each one is a day-1 task)

1. **Paths.** Preview pages live at `site/next/<final-path>/index.html` (e.g. `site/next/cockpit/index.html` → `/next/cockpit/`). Page links are emitted by the builder with `--base /next/`. Assets and API stay root-absolute (D4). The generated-origin files (`/journal/posts.json`, `/public_stats.json`, `/data/character_stats.json`, `/panelcast/episodes.json`, `/board_answers/answers.json`, `/covers/*`) stay root-absolute — no `/next/` behaviour exists for them and none is needed (`web_stack.py:911-1119`).
2. **The hasher (D4).** Test on day 1: run `deploy/sync_site_to_s3.sh --dry-run`, then `python3 deploy/hash_site_assets.py <build copy>` and grep the preview HTML for `/next/assets/` → must be 0 hits and every `/assets/js/v7_*.js` ref must resolve to a file in the build copy.
3. **The chrome pass (D5).** `v4_apply_chrome.py` skips pages without `.doors`/`.site-foot` (`:305-308`) — day-1 test: `python3 scripts/v4_apply_chrome.py --check` after building the preview → exits 0 and reports no change under `site/next/`. `write_page()` still runs `rewrite()` on v7 pages, which applies `v4_glossary.apply_glossary` (`v4_apply_chrome.py:239`) — wanted (the `<dfn class="gloss">` on first use is rubric item 3). The footer/loop-forward/head insertions are gated on the page being chrome-bearing — "a page with a doors nav OR a canonical footer is a content page" (`v4_apply_chrome.py:228-232`; the loop-forward branch raises rather than guesses, `:169-243`) — so a v7 page with neither class is glossed and otherwise left alone. Confirmed by the same `--check` run on Monday.
4. **Registries that walk `site/**`.** Four gates count every `site/**/index.html` and will see the preview tree the moment it is committed:
   - `tests/qa_manifest.py::self_check` (`tests/test_qa_manifest.py:30-34`) → add `EXEMPT["/next/"] = "v7 preview, dated, struck at cut-over"` next to `EXEMPT["/legacy/"]` (`qa_manifest.py:976-977`; prefixes ending in `/` are honoured, `:1301`). The nine get real manifest entries (with `intent` for the comprehension judge) on cut-over day, replacing the old rows.
   - `tests/test_site_orphans.py::test_every_page_is_linked_or_explicitly_unlisted` (`:108-115`) → `/next/` (the preview home) is an orphan by construction; add it to `UNLISTED_PAGES` (`:37-40`) with the dated reason; the other eight are linked from it. `test_unlisted_allowlist_stays_honest` (`:118-124`) then requires that nothing else links `/next/` — keep it that way (the owner gets the URL by message, never a link).
   - `tests/site_text.reader_pages()` (`:40-48`) feeds the vocabulary residue (`tests/site_vocabulary_residue.py`) and the reach ratchet. Reach is a BFS from `site/index.html` (`site_text.py:130-146`), so an unlinked `/next/` tree adds nothing to the 25. The residue ledger is shrink-only per term: **the preview pages must carry zero unglossed registry terms from their first commit** (that is the design anyway: CONCEPT §2 rule 5).
   - `scripts/v4_build_sitemap.py::registry_urls` (`:122-135`) derives from the manifest and drops pages whose own HTML says `noindex` (`:59-67`) — with the EXEMPT prefix and the meta, the preview never enters `sitemap.xml`.
5. **Search engines.** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex,nofollow">` in the v7 template while `--base != /`; `Disallow: /next/` appended to `site/robots.txt:1-5`. There is no `X-Robots-Tag` header anywhere in CDK today (grep of `cdk/stacks`); adding a response-headers policy for `/next/*` would be a CDK deploy — not needed.
6. **The service worker.** `boot_sw.js` registers `/sw.js` at scope `/` and only the cockpit island loads it (`site/sw.js:15-24`; `boot_sw.js:2`). Navigations and `/api/*` are fresh-first, hashed assets cache-first (`sw.js:44-88`). The preview pages do not include `boot_sw.js`; the live SW's rules are already correct for `/next/` navigations. Decide at cut-over whether the v7 Today page keeps the PWA island (recommend yes, unchanged).
7. **404.** The bucket's website `ErrorDocument` is `site/404.html`, bucket-wide, not in CDK (`deploy/smoke_test_site.sh:454-458`) — a mistyped preview URL renders the live 404. Fine.
8. **Deploys.** The preview reaches S3 only through `site-deploy.yml` on a merge to `main` touching `site/**` (auto, no approval gate; `concurrency.group: site-deploy, cancel-in-progress: false`, `site-deploy.yml:88-91`). ### 6c. The ADR to file (draft — one paragraph, numbered on the day)

> **ADR-157 — The site is one serialised investigation: nine reachable pages, the rest served unlisted, and the ratchet holds.** *Context.* Three weeks into cycle 17 the subject had not opened averagejoematt.com and first-time readers scored its doors 2–3 of 5 on the ten-second test; the 2026-09-26 red team (five personas) found the honest receipts — 16 counted starts, a 0-for-5 coach left on the board, an 80 % interval that held 79 %, one subscriber — hidden under fourteen screens of the site describing its own loop. *Decision.* The public site is organised as a single open investigation, published weekly, into one question: can AI over one ordinary man's own numbers move his life, and by how much. Exactly nine pages are reachable from `/` — Home, Today, This week, His numbers, The coaches, What he's trying, Who he is, Under the hood, Follow — plus `/privacy/`; `NAV_REACH_CEILING` is 10 as `static_reach()` counts and may only fall. Every other URL stays served and unlinked (no deletions, no 301s); every page opens on one served fact with its date and range; the coaches appear as expert witnesses with memory — each read opened by its own checked prior claim, the record printed as "K of N through <day>" from one producer, never a bare percentage — and the subject's own dated sentence and photo lead the site. The order rule is fixed: the human first, the counted failures second, the mechanism third, the coaches last and short, the machine vocabulary never. *Consequences.* The reach ratchet, the vocabulary registry, the page manifest and the comprehension judge are the instruments that keep this true; a new reachable page requires lowering nothing and adding an argued exemption to this ADR; the engine gains five served fields (the ledger line, one calibration producer, the morning note, the session, `public_ask`) and changes no number to make a page simpler (ADR-104/105 unchanged). Supersedes the page-count half of the #4182 rulings (v6 §3 vi) and amends ADR-071's five-door IA.
