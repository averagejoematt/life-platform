* **Edge:** each object's public address fetched without following redirects, then with. 43 addresses already answer

  29 answer 200. Every object is ALSO readable at `<address>index.html` (the function only matches directory forms) and
  at the S3 REST endpoint (`site/*` is anonymously readable) — a 301 alone never took a page off the web.
* **Inbound links:** every page in the live sitemaps (`sitemap-0.xml` + `sitemap-site.xml`, 691 pages: the v9 build and
  the kept v5 pages) fetched and scanned, plus the repo's `site/` tree, `www/src`, and the 72 pages themselves. **No
  current page links any of the 72.** The only inbound links are from the noindex `/legacy/` archive and from the 72
  among themselves.
* **Sitemaps and feeds:** `sitemap.xml`, `sitemap-0.xml`, `sitemap-index.xml`, `sitemap-site.xml`, `rss.xml`, `feed.xml`
  list none of the 72.
* **Policy:** `python3 scripts/check_public_copies.py --check` (rule C: the build scan's publication members against a
  fresh live snapshot) reported nothing on these pages; each page was then read, because the scan is necessary, not
  sufficient. No page carries a body photo, a withheld coach-call wording, journal or State of Mind text, a computed age

  restricted prose (named by category below); none is linked or indexed.
* **Chronicle → week and successor:** `/chronicle/` held the pilot and cycle-17 copies of a prior cycle's installments
  (pilot weeks 0–4, two special editions, a sample, a template), tombstoned at the 2026-09-06 restart. The current

  evidence page under `/data/`, `/method/` or `/protocols/` with the same slug; every destination answers 200 and is a
